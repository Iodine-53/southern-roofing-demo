SYMPTOM CHECK

Five signs your roof is telling on itself.

  1. Water ponds for 48 hours after rain. Ponding water finds every seam. Two dry days and it's still sitting there? The slope is failing. Open a work order →
  2. Seams are lifting at the edges. If a fingernail fits under a lifted seam, the wind will fit the whole sheet next. Open a work order →
  3. Ceiling stains after every storm. The stain you see is the last 10% of the problem. The rest is sitting in your insulation. Open a work order →
  4. Leaks at every drain. Drains are where commercial roofs fail first. One recurring drain leak means the flashing has given up. Open a work order →
  5. The roof is older than 15 years. Built-up and modified roofs in Tampa sun live 15–20 years. Past that, every storm is a bet. Open a work order →

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions every facilities manager asks.

How much does a commercial re-roof cost in Tampa?

Most run $6–$14 per sq ft depending on tear-off vs recover, deck condition, and system. A 20,000 sq ft tear-off typically lands in six figures — which is exactly why we core-cut first and quote in writing within 48 hours, with the number fixed.

Can you recover instead of tearing off?

Yes, if the deck and insulation are dry. A recover skips the tear-off and often costs half as much. If the core cut finds wet insulation or two roof systems already stacked, we won't recover it — that's a roof built to fail.

How long does a commercial re-roof take?

Recover systems: 1–2 weeks. Tear-offs: 2–4 weeks. Metal retrofits: 3–5 weeks. You stay open — we stage around your loading docks and forklifts, and the building never sits exposed overnight.

What warranty do you offer?

Every re-roof carries a 10-year workmanship warranty in writing. Manufacturer system warranties up to 20 years are available on TPO and metal systems.

Do you work during business hours?

Yes — most of our work happens while you're open. We coordinate noisy tear-off days around your schedule, stage materials off your docks, and leave the site broom-clean every night.
